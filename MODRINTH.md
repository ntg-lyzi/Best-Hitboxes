# Best Hitboxes

Removes the **blue line** and **red eye box** from F3+B hitboxes. You can also **change the hitbox colour** and **turn on a glow**.

## Features

- **Clean hitboxes.** Only the box is shown. No blue line and no red eye box.
- **Any colour.** Use the colour picker or pick one of the **8 presets**.
- **Glow.** Turn on a glow around the hitbox and set how strong it is.
- **Glow key.** Press **N** to turn the glow on or off.
- **Menu.** Press **P** to open the menu. All settings are on one screen.
- **Change keys.** You can change both keys in the menu or in **Options > Controls**.
- **Easy to turn off.** Turn the mod off in the menu to get normal hitboxes back.

## How to use

1. Press **F3 + B** to show hitboxes.
2. Press **P** to open the menu.
3. Pick a colour and turn on glow if you want.

Your settings save on their own.

## FAQ

**Does it work on servers?**
Yes. It only changes what you see, so it works on any server.

**Do I need it on the server?**
No. It is client side only.

**Why do two things open when I press P?**
P is also the vanilla key for Social Interactions. Change one of them in the menu or in **Options > Controls**.
